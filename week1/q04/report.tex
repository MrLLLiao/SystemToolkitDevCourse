\section{Result}

The following equation shows a simple calculation of the total
latency. As shown in Equation~\eqref{eq:latency}, the total latency
is the sum of the individual latency values.

\begin{equation}
L_{\mathrm{total}} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} L_i
\label{eq:latency}
\end{equation}

Table~\ref{tab:result} summarizes the example results.

\begin{table}[htbp]

\end{table}
